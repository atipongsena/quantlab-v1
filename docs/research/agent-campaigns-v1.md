# QuantLab Autonomous Research Campaigns & Multi-Agent Specifications (V1)

## 1. Collaborative Research Hierarchy
1. **Idea Generator**: Proposes economic hypothesis based on market anomalies and risk premia.
2. **Factor Engineer**: Constructs mathematical factor expressions and rolling features.
3. **Adversarial Validation Critic**: Applies 4-vector falsification:
   - Information leakage & PIT verification.
   - Overfitting & Multiple testing Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR $\ge 0.80$).
   - Parameter sensitivity topology (rejecting fragile spikes).
   - Execution friction stress & turnover feasibility.
4. **Lead Strategist**: Synthesizes agent evaluations, issues authoritative verdicts (`VALIDATED` / `REJECTED`), and publishes immutable cryptographic research reports.

## 2. Cryptographic Report Integrity
- Research reports are hashed with SHA-256 and can be verified offline via `quantlab report verify <path>`.
